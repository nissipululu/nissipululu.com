About

I'm Nissi Pululu, a commercial photographer in Toronto, and I got into this because I got mad.

In 2018 I needed a photographer. I was desperate, and every quote I got was terrible. So I started shooting myself. Around then I met Kevin Kosi, who became my best friend and is now my cofounder at Sonset Inc.

In the studio I'm a perfectionist. I keep adjusting until every detail is right, and I'm usually the first to spot what's off. I guess I have a good eye. I've pointed it at some of the largest organizations and businesses in Canada.

Most people only look at the front of a picture. I get to look at both sides. I know what I put in the frame, and I know what hides in the files without being asked. That second side is why Sonset Inc. exists. We build visual systems for a post AI world, so a picture can speak for itself, with a label and a number to confirm it.

When I'm not working I'm probably swimming, or at a Raptors, Blue Jays or Maple Leafs game.

I would be nowhere without my love for Jesus. He guides my path, so if you believe in him too, I think we could do great work together.

I'm a wholesome guy who wants to create visually appealing images and push my ideas. I want to help make the world a better place as I learn and grow.
