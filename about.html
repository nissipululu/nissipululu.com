Mission

The internet is drowning in synthetic imagery. The cost of generating a "good enough" photo has collapsed to zero. The premium has shifted from creation to verification, signal, and narrative coherence.

I operate at that intersection. My work focuses on capturing high fidelity visual data, moments, textures, and human truths that algorithms can't hallucinate, and packaging them into narrative assets that scale.

I'm not here to add to the noise. I'm here to build the moat.

Currently building PhotoRush, a live platform where someone books a photographer, pays by card, and receives their photos in one flow, the first proof that visual infrastructure can be productized, not just freelanced.
