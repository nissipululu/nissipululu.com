Vision

The next threat to a companys reputation will be digital media. Its already happening. A single file can take down your servers. Hidden in an email, a video, or a pitch deck.

Every picture within an enterprise is a small window, opened by accident. Businesses need to know, for each image, video or file they hold should be organized by what it is, who made it and what may be done with it.

Vital information can be stolen all because no systems were implemented for protection.

Files should be assessed elsewhere safely before human looks at them. Nothing should leave a company carrying more than it was meant to say.

Im a commercial photographer. I build the infrastructure for corporate and international enterprises, offering visual privacy and protection from malicious attacks and digital espionage.

A picture should be able to speak for itself, with a label and a number to confirm it.

I build these systems because I see both sides of a picture. I know what I put in the frame, I know what hides in the files without being asked. You guarded your words and forgot the media, the thing people trust most.

Machines posses replicant abilities, trust has moved from whats visible to whats real and can be checked. I am building the visual infrastructure for that world.
