Envy showed up to picture day with roses and absolutely no apologies. The olive backdrop, the monogram sneakers, the rings catching the light, every detail is deliberate without feeling overdressed, luxurious without feeling cold. This is a man who knows exactly how he wants to be remembered, and he made sure the camera agreed. Part portrait, part flex, entirely his own.
