La Vie En Noir

August 13, 2026

Photographed by Nissi Pululu

I wanted to strip everything away and focus only on the shapes the human body can make. Diana Balinka is wearing a simple black bodysuit and heels against a completely white backdrop, which makes her silhouette pop like ink on paper. The story is about the strength in simplicity; there are no props or busy colors to hide behind. I used bright, even lighting to keep the background seamless and to make sure the sharp angles of her pose were perfectly defined. It is a classic look that proves you don't need a complicated set to create a powerful image, just a clear vision and a subject who knows how to move.
