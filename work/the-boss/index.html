The Boss

August 13, 2026

Photographed by Nissi Pululu

I turned the studio into a dark room, controlling the light from all angles. Paul Ufeli stood in this manufactured darkness, wearing a navy suit that blended with the entire composition. I darkened the scene to enhance the shadows and make the white clothes glow, creating a powerful environment. We tried different poses but these powerful images showcase the best ones. The model is the only solid thing the stood out with a little light and no color.
